# Lazy-eye anaglyph study mode

## Why
Learners with anisometropic / amblyopic training needs can study flashcards through dichoptic (anaglyph) presentation calibrated to their glasses and screen.

## What changes
- Header toggle: Regular ↔ Lazy eye (web + mobile)
- Profile settings for named anaglyph glass profiles (per-device colors + background)
- Strong eye + weaken controls on glass profiles (web + mobile); study dims that eye toward the background
- Study text: letters for word/POS/IPA; syllables for examples/definition
- Rating buttons: vertical split colors + soft alternating pulse
